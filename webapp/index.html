How it works

Three steps, no setup.

  1. 01

    Allow the camera

    Your browser asks once. The video is processed right there and never leaves your device.

  2. 02

    Sign naturally

    21 points on each hand — plus your arms and shoulders for sentences — are tracked many times a second.

  3. 03

    Read it, hear it

    Letters, numbers and sentences appear on screen, are spoken aloud and saved to your history.
